I am a robotics researcher and lifelong learner. I received my Ph.D. in Human Informatics from the University of Tsukuba in March 2023. Since August 2026, I have been working as a Chief Research Scientist at SB Intuitions, focusing on Physical AI. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL from April 2025 to July 2026, supervised by Prof. Dario Floreano; a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Tsukuba from April 2024 to March 2025; a visiting postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich from June 2023 to December 2023; and a research fellow (PD) of the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) from April 2023 to March 2024. My research interests include Physical AI, aerial robotics, human augmentation, human-robot interaction, teleoperation, shared control, and assistive technology.

Research goal: Use robotics technology to empower humans.
